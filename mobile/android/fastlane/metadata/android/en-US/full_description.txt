NomadIQ is with you before, during and after your trip, with Flyra, its AI assistant.

✈️ FLIGHTS
• Search flights and see prices from Aviasales (Travelpayouts).
• Follow a route and get notified when the price drops.
• Explore destinations by month and climate, and discover airline stopover programs.

🗺️ AT YOUR DESTINATION
• 3D map and audio guides that start on their own as you enter each area, even offline.
• Camera: point at a landmark and hear its story.
• Near you: water, toilets, wifi, ATMs, transport, events and hour-by-hour rain.
• Restaurant menu translator and voice interpreter.

👥 AS A GROUP
• Pick destination and dates together with Flyra's help.
• Trip chat, plans with votes and meetups with other travellers.
• Shared expenses with receipt scanner and currency converter.

🛡️ PEACE OF MIND
• Encrypted vault for your documents, only on your phone.
• SOS with the country's emergency number, even without internet.
• Visas with official links, delay claims (EU261) and what to do if your bag is lost.

🔒 PRIVACY
Your documents, photos and memories stay on your phone. No ads. We don't sell data. You can delete your account from the app.
