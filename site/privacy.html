NomadIQ privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

In short: we keep the minimum the app needs to work. Many things (your documents, photos, memories, medical card, budget) stay only on your phone and never reach us. There are no ads, we use no third-party analytics and we do not sell data. You can delete your account and everything we hold from inside the app.

1. Who is responsible

NomadIQ is developed by an independent developer based in Spain. For any privacy question write to __SUPPORT_EMAIL__.

2. What stays only on your phone (never sent to us)

  • The Vault: photos of passports, insurance and documents (encrypted on the phone).
  • Passport data read from the MRZ (the Visa screen stores only your country).
  • Receipt and restaurant menu photos: the text is read on the phone.
  • Your Memories (photos, videos, voice notes) and your stamp passport.
  • The SOS medical card, your bank cards and your budget.
  • Your Flyra chat history, packing list and offline downloads.

Our server logs do not store your IP address, the paths you request or your coordinates: only which service answered, with which status and how fast.

4. Who it is shared with

  • Oracle Cloud (our servers, in the European Union) and Oracle Object Storage (backups, encrypted before they leave our server).
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers notifications. Their content is end-to-end encrypted; Google cannot read it.
  • When you open a booking link (flights, stays, transport, tickets) you leave the app for that site (for example Aviasales/Travelpayouts, Booking.com or the airline) and its privacy policy applies. We may earn a commission if you book; your price does not change.
  • Your phone downloads maps (OpenFreeMap), Wikimedia Commons photos and Mapillary street photos directly: those sites see your IP address and the area you view, as with any website.
  • Voice dictation uses your Android speech recogniser which, depending on your phone, may process audio on Google's servers.

We never sell or rent data, and there is no advertising.

5. Security

Everything travels encrypted (TLS 1.3). Inside our servers each service proves its identity with a certificate (mTLS) and only talks to the ones it needs. Passwords are stored as Argon2id hashes and backups are encrypted.

6. Your rights

You can access and take your data (Settings → Download my data), correct it, object to its use and delete it (Settings → Delete my account and my data, see how to delete your account). For anything else write to __SUPPORT_EMAIL__. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in Spain, the AEPD: aepd.es).

The legal basis is providing the service you request when you use each feature and, for location, camera, microphone and notifications, your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in your phone settings.

7. Age

NomadIQ is meant for people aged 18 and over (it includes meetups and chats with other travellers). We do not knowingly collect data from minors; if you believe a minor has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

8. Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you inside the app.
