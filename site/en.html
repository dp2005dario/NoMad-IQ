ECLIPTA

Your next trip is already taking off. A travel assistant with Flyra, a map, audio guides, flights and group trips.

Status: ECLIPTA is in development and testing. It is not yet published on Google Play. Some features need a connection to our server.

What it does

  • Flyra, your assistantA chat with Flyra, the app's AI, to ask about your trip. It also answers inside a group trip chat. It needs a connection: our server replies.
  • Map and audio guidesA map with points of interest and audio guides that start when you enter a zone (with travel mode, if you turn it on). You can download cities to use offline.
  • Landmark cameraPoint at a landmark and the app tells you what it is and narrates it. It uses your position and the compass; the photo is processed and discarded.
  • FlightsPrice-drop alerts for the routes you follow, a fare search and fare map, and the flight route. When you book you go to the airline's or the search site's web.
  • Group tripsA trip chat with Flyra, plans you vote on by swiping cards, and a negotiation between everyone's preferences.
  • Shared expensesPots to split costs, with ticket reading on your phone and currency conversion.
  • Encrypted vaultYour documents (passport, insurance) encrypted on your phone and protected by fingerprint. They never reach our server.
  • Papers and safetyPassport and visa check by destination, your rights in case of delay (EU regulation 261) and an SOS button with the country's emergency numbers.
  • And moreRestaurant menu translator, airport help, queues, Android widgets, and the app is translated into 25 languages.

Affiliate links and how we are funded

ECLIPTA is free and has no ads. Some booking links (flights, stays) may be affiliate links, for example Travelpayouts/Aviasales or Booking.com: if you book through them we may receive a commission at no extra cost to you. The app does not make up prices: it takes you to the seller's site, where you see the real price.

Privacy

Many things (documents, photos, memories, medical card) stay only on your phone. There is no advertising, we use no third-party analytics and we do not sell data. You can delete your account from the app.
