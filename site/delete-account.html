How to delete your NomadIQ account

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open NomadIQ and tap the circle with your initial (top of Home) to open Settings.
  2. Tap Delete my account and my data.
  3. If you like, download a copy of your data first.
  4. Enter your password to confirm.

Without the app or your password

Write to __SUPPORT_EMAIL__ from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". That is how we know the account is yours. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email and password) and all your sessions.
  • Your flight alerts and routes, recommendations and phones registered for notifications.
  • Your shared trips, card leagues, meetups you proposed, and your Radar and queue reports.
  • Your group chat messages and your trip preferences.
  • From the app, also everything stored on the phone (Vault, Memories, settings).

What is kept (without your name)

  • In group expenses and group trips with other people, your seat stays but anonymous (no account, shown as "—"), so the others' balances stay correct.
  • Encrypted backups rotate on their own: your data disappears from them within 14 days.
